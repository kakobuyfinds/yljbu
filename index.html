Blank amounts are excluded. Confirm the final quote after the parcel is measured and weighed.

What to check before ordering

  1. Save the seller link and variant. Keep the item ID, color, size, and current seller price together so you can find the same listing later.
  2. Compare measurements in centimeters. For clothing, measure a garment you already own laid flat. Check whether the seller’s chart lists flat width or full circumference.
  3. Review the warehouse QC photos when available. Match the selected variant, confirm the quantity, and inspect visible details. Ask for a ruler photo if a measurement affects fit.
  4. Compare parcel quotes for your destination. Check which weight the shipping line uses for billing and whether tracking, insurance, and taxes are included.

Frequently asked questions

What is this iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent shopping guide with a searchable selection of products from HipobuyFind. Each product links to its HipobuyFind detail page.

Can I use a listing with iTaoBuy?

Open the product detail page, find its original seller link, and check whether your agent accepts that seller link and the selected variant.

What is the difference between listing photos and QC photos?

Listing photos illustrate the item offered by the seller. Warehouse QC photos, when available, show the unit received by the agent and can help you check color, size labels, and visible condition.
